# HLS observational context

`vegetation_context.json` preserves all 191 2025 observations, quality flags, NDVI quartiles, pixel counts and valid/covered fractions, plus the six supplied seasonal summaries. Of these, 94 observations are usable. The remaining observations are missing or low quality, never treated as zero vegetation. NDVI is a satellite spectral index, not percent ground cover, crop yield, or a measured outcome for any proposed rotation. The scenario's planned living-cover index and observed HLS context remain separate.
