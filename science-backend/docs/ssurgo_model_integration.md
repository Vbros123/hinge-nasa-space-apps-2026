# SSURGO capacity interpretation

`engine/soil_adapter.py` multiplies each mapunit's clipped AOI fraction by its component fraction. It checks fractions, finite positive storage, the 100 cm target and component/mapunit correspondence. It preserves missing component mass rather than assigning it to observed components. Known weighted mass: 0.8924041135; unknown: 0.1075958865. Conditional, weighted, discrete 10th/50th/90th percentiles of *known* 100 cm component storage produce 97.0/129.3/170.0 mm sensitivity capacities. These are scenario points across survey components, not uncertainty confidence limits, unique field means, measured moisture, or crop rooting depth. Profiles and restriction types require agronomist review. Initial bucket storage defaults to 50% of each capacity and is tested through the existing spin-up/initialization parameter sets.
